3. Why We Process Your Data

We process personal and health data for the following purposes:

Coherence tracking and reporting: To generate longitudinal coherence reports that track how a client's physiological systems interact and evolve over time. This is the core service provided by MANA³.

Practitioner intelligence: To provide practitioners with data-driven insights that support their clinical decision-making in guiding clients toward their health, performance, and longevity goals.

Platform operation: To maintain client accounts, synchronize wearable data, process daily check-ins, and deliver reports.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (nFADP), we process your data on the following legal bases:

Explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR): Health data is classified as a special category of personal data. We process this data only with the client's explicit, informed consent, obtained during onboarding. This consent covers the collection of wearable device data, practitioner assessment data, and daily check-in data.

Contractual necessity (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): Processing of account information is necessary to provide the MANA³ service as agreed between the practitioner, client, and platform.

5. Wearable Device Data

MANA³ integrates with wearable device platforms including Garmin (via the Garmin Health API), Polar (via the Polar AccessLink API), and Oura (via the Oura API) to collect wellness and activity data.

When a client connects their wearable device to MANA³, they authorize the device manufacturer to share their data with our platform. This authorization can be revoked at any time through the device manufacturer's settings or by contacting us directly.

We do not sell, share, or transfer wearable device data to any third party. Data received from wearable device APIs is used exclusively for generating coherence reports within the MANA³ platform and is accessible only to the client and their assigned practitioner.

We do not store raw API responses beyond what is necessary to compute coherence scores. Processed data is stored as normalized anchor values (sleep score, readiness score, stress proxy) without retaining the full device data payload.

6. AI and Data Processing

MANA³ uses an AI-assisted narrative engine to generate interpretive reports for practitioners based on pre-computed coherence scores. The following details describe how AI is used within the platform and how it relates to data obtained from wearable device APIs, including the Garmin Health API.

AI is used in the application. MANA³ employs a large language model (LLM) via the Anthropic Claude API to generate narrative interpretations of coherence data. The AI receives pre-computed numerical scores — not raw API data — and produces written summaries that help practitioners understand trends, anomalies, and relationships across the twelve fields of the coherence model.

Garmin data is involved in this processing, but only as pre-computed scores. Data obtained from the Garmin Health API (sleep scores, stress levels, Body Battery, HRV, training load) is first processed deterministically by our scoring engine into normalized anchor values. These numerical scores — not the raw Garmin API data — are then passed to the AI narrative engine for interpretation. The AI does not receive raw Garmin API responses, device identifiers, or any personally identifiable information.

No Garmin data is used to train, fine-tune, or improve any external AI or LLM model. The AI is accessed exclusively via API on a per-request basis. No client data, Garmin data, or any data processed by MANA³ is used to train, fine-tune, retrain, or otherwise improve any AI model, machine learning system, or large language model — whether operated by Anthropic, by MANA³, or by any third party. Data sent to the AI API is processed ephemerally and is not retained by the AI provider for training purposes.

All scoring is deterministic. The coherence scores, gap calculations, and anomaly detection are computed by deterministic rule-based systems (not AI). The AI component is limited to narrative generation — translating numerical scores into human-readable practitioner reports. The AI does not make clinical decisions, diagnoses, or recommendations.

7. How We Store Your Data

Client data is stored in a secure database with access restricted to the assigned practitioner and the MANA³ platform. We implement the following security measures:

Access control: Each client's data is accessible only to their assigned practitioner and to the MANA³ system for report generation. No other practitioners, clients, or third parties can access the data.

Encryption: Data is encrypted in transit (TLS/SSL) and at rest where supported by the hosting infrastructure.

Data minimization: We collect only the data necessary to generate coherence reports. We do not collect GPS location data, social data, or any data beyond what is described in Section 2.

8. Who Has Access to Your Data

Access to your data is strictly limited to:

Your assigned practitioner: The practitioner you have been paired with through the MANA³ platform. They access your data to provide personalized guidance.

The MANA³ platform: Automated systems that process your data to generate coherence scores and reports. This includes an AI-assisted narrative engine that produces interpretive summaries of your data for your practitioner.

We do not share your data with advertisers, data brokers, research institutions, or any other third party. We do not use your data for any purpose other than delivering the MANA³ service.

9. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as you are an active client on the platform. If you or your practitioner terminate the service, we will delete your personal and health data within 30 days of receiving a deletion request, unless we are legally required to retain it for a longer period.

Anonymized, aggregated data that cannot be traced back to any individual may be retained for the purpose of improving the platform's coherence models.

10. Your Rights

Under the GDPR and Swiss nFADP, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

Right of access: You can request a copy of all personal data we hold about you.

Right to rectification: You can request correction of inaccurate data.

Right to erasure: You can request deletion of your data at any time. This includes revocation of wearable device authorization.

Right to data portability: You can request your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.

Right to withdraw consent: You can withdraw your consent to data processing at any time. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.

Right to lodge a complaint: You have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. For EU residents, this is the relevant Data Protection Authority in your member state. For Swiss residents, this is the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below.

11. International Data Transfers

MANA³ operates from Switzerland, which benefits from an EU adequacy decision for data protection. When wearable device data is transferred from device manufacturers (who may store data outside Switzerland or the EU), this transfer is governed by the data processing agreements between the client and the device manufacturer (e.g., Garmin, Polar, Oura) and their respective privacy policies.

We may update this privacy policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Any significant changes will be communicated to active clients and their practitioners. The date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last updated.
